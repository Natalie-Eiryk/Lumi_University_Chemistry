# Ms. Luminara: Chemistry Adventures, Expanded Edition

Five familiar journeys, given room to unfold.

Begin with [CONTENTS.md](CONTENTS.md). Each adventure can be read straight through. Vocabulary support, untimed practice and reasoned answers sit beside the story, so a discovery never depends on passing a quiz.

This edition builds on the five chemistry stories from the First Collection. Their stable identities and twenty original practice questions are retained. New questions extend the same ideas through fresh situations. The earlier ethics and cross-subject stories remain in their original collection.

The chapters are original concept-reinforcement fiction for personal study. They are not instructor-approved answers, a complete course summary, a lab report, or a submission for assessment. A source worksheet establishes a topic's context, not an answer key or a current exam boundary. Follow the instructor's current assistance policy for submitted work.

## Reading choices

- **Story:** open a chapter's STORY.md and travel with the class.
- **Support:** open SUPPORT.md for terminology, symbols and useful distinctions before, during or after reading.
- **Practice:** choose any question in PRACTICE.md; hints and references are welcome.
- **Explanation:** TEACHER_KEY.md provides worked reasoning without requiring an attempt first.
- **Vocabulary cards:** each chapter includes twelve new cards linked to scenes, support and practice; the full deck is in study_support.
- **One-file reader:** the readers folder combines each story with its learner companions. Answer keys remain separate.

The characters' dialogue is fiction. Scientific claims are grounded in each chapter's SOURCES.md. Metaphors, imaginative travel and historical reconstructions are distinguished from evidence.

## Preservation and integration

Each original chapter and each of three revision passes has its own ZIP checkpoint. The companion checkpoint-history archive preserves the exact ZIP bytes. The expanded chapters are reviewable authoring content, not a claim of canonical Atlas approval, installation, runtime validation or publication.

For later integration, begin with [CODEX_INTEGRATION.md](CODEX_INTEGRATION.md). Keep the existing University identity, source records, practice IDs and shared Save flow. No application changes are included in this delivery.
