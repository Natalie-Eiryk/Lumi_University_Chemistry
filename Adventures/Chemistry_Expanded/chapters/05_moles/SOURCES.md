## Model and precision boundaries

- Story isotope data: artificial 10 u and 11 u assignments, with all displayed mixtures and sample sizes authored for illustration.
- Preserved q03/q04 and q12: artificial 24 u and 26 u assignments. They are not real element data.
- Isotope fractions used in atomic means are atom-number fractions. Equal mass fractions generally differ from equal atom fractions.
- A weighted mean need not equal an individual value. A noninteger individual real isotope mass is also possible. Neither claim licenses fractional neutron counts.
- N = nN_A and m = nM have different jobs. A constituent count also needs the entity's composition. Algebraic unit cancellation alone does not identify the chemical species.
- Actual particles are not visible hard balls, and a representation can omit individual members while preserving count relationships. The station's containers encode no gas-law volume, temperature, pressure, or density.
- A formula unit in the supplied ionic-solid model is composition accounting, not an isolated molecule. Counting constituent atoms does not mean freeing them from molecules or neutralizing ions.
- Exact thought-experiment amounts are labeled. Approximate molar masses and measured input precision remain approximate. The tiny post-2019 atomic-mass/molar-mass numerical distinction is reserved for optional support, not a core demand.
