## S05 Formula roles and molecular composition

## Limits of the scientific models

- A schematic lattice is not a measured crystal structure or a collection of exclusive molecular couples.
- A neutral formula and recognized name do not prove synthesis, phase, stability, solubility, safety, or every aspect of bonding.
- Common charge patterns and metal/nonmetal/metalloid clues have declared introductory scope. Verified identity takes precedence over a shortcut. A compositional name alone is not proof of a particular bonding structure.
- Polyatomic ions retain their internal composition and overall charge. A conventional lowest whole-ion ratio is not permission to divide all atom subscripts indiscriminately.
- Molecular formulas preserve atoms per specified molecule. Empirical ratios and total inventories are distinct requested descriptions.
- Equation-like notation in the story is charge or composition bookkeeping unless explicitly described otherwise. No real experimental operations are taught.
- Available references and correct worked examples do not constitute automated grading validation or an instructor's certification.

All external scientific prose is newly paraphrased; no textbook exercise, figure, image, or passage is reproduced. Source works retain their own rights.
