# Sources, continuity and coverage

Prepared 8 October 2026.

## What was expanded

The five chemistry adventures in the 7 October First Collection are the narrative baseline. Original titles, central puzzles, scientific boundaries and practice identities are retained; the longer scenes and new study material are newly authored. This is an expansion of that collection, not a replacement for every chemistry lesson, worksheet or earlier adventure.

| Adventure | Core investigation | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 001, The Address With No Row | Periodic clues, electron configurations and what evidence identifies an element | Neutral, ground-state main-group atoms among the first twenty for the main identification puzzle |
| 002, The Bracket Luggage Office | Reversible noble-gas shorthand, counts, occupancy and ions | Selected first-twenty configurations and simple ions; no universal transition-metal rule |
| 003, The City Without Couples | Extended ionic structures, molecules, polyatomic ions and formula units | Ordinary solid NaCl and explicitly scoped introductory bonding models |
| 004, The Compound Naming Station | Two-way translation among species, formulas and names | Declared introductory naming conventions, not the entirety of chemical nomenclature |
| 005, The Mole Counting Station | Specified entities, amount, mass and isotope averages | Exact defining constant, approximate supplied molar masses, explicitly artificial isotope models |

The separate ethics and cross-subject stories in the original collection are not rewritten here. The earlier connected *Loom of Chemical Meaning* adventure is a distinct work, with its own identity and activity map.
