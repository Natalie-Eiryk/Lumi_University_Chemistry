# New optional vocabulary cards

These 60 retrieval cards are newly authored supplements for the expanded edition. They are not recovered cards from the original collection and are separate from the twenty original practice questions. Each chapter has four definition, four contrast, and four application cards.

Use them only if they help. Speak, write, sketch with labels, consult a reference, or read an answer immediately. There is no timer, required recall mode, or automatic grade. Accepted-reasoning notes identify the chemistry to recognize, not exact wording. These are private concept-reinforcement materials, not course submissions or evidence of instructor approval.

Scene, support, practice, and source links were checked against the expanded text on 8 October 2026. A “method transfer” note identifies cases in which a scene teaches the method with different supplied numbers or species. The linked practice gives the card’s specific case where noted.

### luminara.adventure.001.card01

**Definition.** For a chlorine atom or ion, what does atomic number 17 count?

**Answer:** The 17 protons in its nucleus. Proton number fixes its identity as chlorine.

**Reasoning to recognize:** A proton-count explanation is sufficient; electrons or mass number do not define atomic number.

### luminara.adventure.005.card01

**Definition.** What quantity does the unit mole measure when the named entities are O₂ molecules?

**Answer:** Amount of substance of those specified O₂ molecules. One mole corresponds to exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ such molecules.
