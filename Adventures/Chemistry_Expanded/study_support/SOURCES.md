## Provenance and boundaries

- The recovered first collection supplies the original titles, story IDs, central puzzles, and q01–q04 identifiers. Expanded chapter companions contain the fuller provenance.
- Configuration prompts stay in their declared first-twenty neutral ground-state main-group context, except when an ion is explicitly supplied. A correct electron total is not enough to establish correct occupancy.
- Charge, subscript, prefix, coefficient, and oxidation-state notation have different jobs. Oxidation state is not universally a localized physical charge.
- Ordinary solid NaCl is an extended lattice. Formula-unit accounting does not assert isolated NaCl molecules.
- Preserve polyatomic ions and use verified identity when an introductory metal/nonmetal shortcut is insufficient. Neutrality alone does not establish stability, synthesis, or existence.
- Molecular formulas retain the atom counts in the specified molecule; empirical formulas answer a different question.
- The isotope values 24 u and 26 u are explicitly artificial teaching values. Their abundances are atom-number fractions. Real isotope masses need not be integers.
- The exact Avogadro constant does not make a measured amount or approximate molar mass exact. The 32.00 g/mol value is supplied at introductory precision, not presented as an exact universal oxygen value.
- Related Unit II authoring IDs identify selected conceptual connections only. The source contains 142 total records across its two files; these five stories do not claim to cover them all. Lewis structures, resonance, geometry, and instructor-sensitive naming choices require additional study.

## Course uncertainty

The recovered Unit II worksheet is titled “Chem. 1117 Review Unit II Chapters 4–5.” Its F25 filename does not prove the current term, a current due date, or instructor policy. The exact four octet exceptions, the meaning of “orbital geometry,” SO₃ representation preference, OsO₄ naming preference, and requested electron-equation formats remain instructor-sensitive. This folder does not settle them or reproduce the worksheet key.


## Source-reference locations in card copies

The central cards.json points to this file’s explicit ref- anchors. A chapter-local vocabulary_cards.json instead points to that chapter’s SOURCES.md sections, while retaining the same direct scientific-source URLs. Each relative reference is interpreted from its containing JSON file’s directory. This routing difference makes the local source trail usable in a self-contained chapter; it does not change the card’s prompt, answer, accepted reasoning, scene binding, or practice binding. See the [copy contract](README.md#central-deck-and-chapter-copy-source-contract).

Local validation checks that the cited file and explicit or Markdown-heading anchor exist in that context. Direct external URLs are scientific references from the source audit; a local-link check is not a claim that every outside website is continuously available.
