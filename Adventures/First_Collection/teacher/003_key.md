# Teacher guide and key for The City Without Couples

Story ID: luminara.adventure.003

## Purpose and prerequisites

Distinguish bond descriptions, species charge, element/compound status, and extended structures. Use charge balance while preserving whole polyatomic ions. Suggested prerequisites: 002 or an equivalent ion ledger. Supply all ion charges; this is not an ion-name memorization test.

## Reasoned key

- **q01:** O₂ is an elemental molecule; H₂O is a molecular compound; NH₄⁺ is a polyatomic ion. All contain covalent bonds. Charge and the number of distinct elements answer questions that a bond label alone does not resolve.
- **q02:** One calcium to two nitrate ions, Ca(NO₃)₂. Charge: +2 + 2(−1) = 0. Counts: Ca 1, N 2, O 6. Parentheses preserve each nitrate's N:O = 1:3 composition while repeating the group twice.
- **q03:** Al₂O₃. Two Al³⁺ total +6; three O²⁻ total −6. This is the simplest neutral ion ratio. A molecular formula records atoms per molecule, so N₂O₄ remains N₂O₄. NO₂ would be a different molecular composition, even though it is the same simplest elemental ratio.
- **q04:** The six are nearest neighbors of one selected ion, shared throughout an extended lattice, not six uniquely assigned partners. The bulk Na:Cl ratio remains 1:1. An optional coordination illustration may also note that each chloride has six nearest sodium neighbors. No unit-cell fractional counting is required.

## Review and feedback

Assess classification dimensions separately. For formulas, review intact ions, total charge, simplest ion ratio, and readable notation. Give the earliest useful repair rather than listing every possible chemical fact. If CaNO₃ is proposed, point out +2 + (−1) = +1 and invite another whole nitrate. If CaN₂O₃ is proposed, ask what the second nitrate brings with it.

Do not teach literal ion “couples,” depict covalent electron sharing as a sodium–chloride pair's permanent transferred token, or imply the lattice model accounts for all bonding nuances. Do not use hazardous demonstrations; all scenes are fictional models. No treatment of a learner's anxiety is inferred from a character's balcony choice.
