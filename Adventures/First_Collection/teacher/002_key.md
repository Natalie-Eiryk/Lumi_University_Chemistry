# Teacher guide and key for The Bracket Luggage Office

Story ID: luminara.adventure.002

## Purpose and prerequisites

Develop reversible shorthand, separate chemistry validity from a formatting objective, and distinguish electron count from element identity. Suggested prior story: 001. Provide addition support and expanded configurations without penalty.

## Reasoned key

- **q01:** [He] 3s² totals four electrons and omits the eight in 2s² 2p⁶. The longer helium-core form expands correctly to twelve. [Ne] 3s² also expands correctly and satisfies the nearest-earlier-core request. Distinguish missing inventory from a valid but longer notation.
- **q02:** Mg²⁺, because 12 − 10 = +2. Twelve protons identifies magnesium, whereas neon has ten. Shared electron count does not establish identical properties or nucleus. No additional ion-property recall is required.
- **q03:** The total-count check passes. The occupancy/ground-state check fails: the given ground-state configuration occupies 4s², not 3d². Useful feedback: “Your total accounts for all twenty. Now compare where the last two are placed with the supplied ground-state reference.”
- **q04:** [He] 2s² 2p⁶. The instruction chooses a preceding-core representation. Writing or expanding that abbreviation changes the description, not the physical species. A bare [Ne] is not the requested demonstration, but should not be described as ten missing electrons.

## Review criteria

For each response, separately check expansion fidelity, total, occupancy where relevant, proton identity, and the requested convention. Mark only applicable criteria. “Not yet shown” is different from incorrect; ambiguous notation should prompt clarification. Accept 3s^2 as an unambiguous occupancy notation in this context, and Mg^2+ for charge. Do not globally strip superscripts, signs, or letter case.

The optional deeper-energy door should remain optional. Do not require orbital-energy derivations to accept the accounting explanation. A student can choose an explanation directly rather than earning it through failure.
