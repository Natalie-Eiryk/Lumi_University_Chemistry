# Transfer questions for the Compound Naming Station

Story ID: luminara.adventure.004

These questions are separate from the adventure. A teacher or learner may select any of them. References, spoken explanations, labeled sketches, calculators, and revision are welcome. If a recall-only check is wanted, agree on that mode beforehand. State reasoning as well as a final name or formula.

## Question 1 Copper at two platforms

ID: luminara.adventure.004.q01

CuCl and CuCl₂ are supplied as simple ionic compounds. Chloride has charge −1.

Name both compounds. Explain what the Roman numeral means in each name and why the subscript 2 in CuCl₂ does not mean two copper ions.

## Question 2 A salt without a metal

ID: luminara.adventure.004.q02

A known ionic compound consists of ammonium ions, NH₄⁺, and carbonate ions, CO₃²⁻.

Write its conventional neutral formula preserving whole ions, give its name, and explain why “no metal means molecular” would fail. Count N, H, C, and O separately in one formula unit's composition.

## Question 3 Choosing a naming language

ID: luminara.adventure.004.q03

MgCl₂ is supplied as a simple ionic compound; Mg²⁺ and Cl⁻ are its ions. PCl₃ and SF₆ are supplied as binary molecular compounds.

Name all three. Choose one numeral or prefix in your answers and explain exactly what it describes. Does magnesium chloride need the molecular prefix di before chloride in this naming convention?

## Question 4 A formula that must keep its passengers

ID: luminara.adventure.004.q04

A machine changes the molecular formula H₂O₂ into HO because both subscripts are divisible by two.

Explain what information the original molecular formula carries, what HO can describe in this context, and why the change is unacceptable when the task asks for the molecular formula. Then state whether N₂O₄ and NO₂ name the same molecular composition.

## Optional reflection

Which check would you make first with an unfamiliar formula? It is valid to say that you would consult a reference before deciding whether an introductory naming rule applies. This reflection is not scored.
