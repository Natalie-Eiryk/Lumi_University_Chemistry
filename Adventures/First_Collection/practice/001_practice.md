# Optional practice for The Address With No Row

Story ID: luminara.adventure.001

Reference-aided reasoning is welcome. Explain by speaking, typing, sketching with labels, or using the supplied counts. There is no timer or automatic grade.

## luminara.adventure.001.q01

Among neutral ground-state atoms in the first twenty elements, the outer pattern ns² np⁴ fits oxygen (2s² 2p⁴) and sulfur (3s² 3p⁴). A delivery system accepts only one answer. Is the clue sufficient? What extra observation would let it choose? Explain rather than guessing.

## luminara.adventure.001.q02

A species has seventeen protons and eighteen electrons. Someone identifies it as argon because its electron count is eighteen. Use the supplied atomic numbers, Cl = 17 and Ar = 18, to repair the claim and state its charge. Does the story's original neutral-atom clue apply?

## luminara.adventure.001.q03

A learner says the 5 in 3p⁵ means five electrons in the entire atom. Explain the scope of the 5. Then count all electrons in 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁵. What additional condition allows that electron count to identify the atomic number?

## luminara.adventure.001.q04

Write two sentences distinguishing a useful group pattern from a claim that every member behaves identically. You may use a non-chemical analogy, but state where it stops explaining chemistry.
