# Optional practice for The City Without Couples

Story ID: luminara.adventure.003

These are explanation and transfer tasks, not a compulsory ending. References and support remain available.

## luminara.adventure.003.q01

Classify O₂, H₂O, and NH₄⁺ using the supplied facts: O₂ and H₂O are neutral discrete molecules; NH₄⁺ is a charged bonded group. Which is elemental, which is a molecular compound, and which is a polyatomic ion? Explain why “has covalent bonds” is not a complete classification.

## luminara.adventure.003.q02

Build neutral calcium nitrate from Ca²⁺ and NO₃⁻. State the ion ratio, formula, and total numbers of Ca, N, and O represented. Explain the parentheses.

## luminara.adventure.003.q03

Build the simplest neutral ionic formula from Al³⁺ and O²⁻. Show the charge accounting. Then explain why the instruction to reduce an ionic ratio does not authorize changing the molecular formula N₂O₄ to NO₂.

## luminara.adventure.003.q04

In ordinary solid NaCl, an illustration shows one sodium ion surrounded by six nearest chloride neighbors. A student concludes the compound must be NaCl₆. Identify the difference between a local neighborhood and an overall composition ratio. A text explanation is sufficient.
