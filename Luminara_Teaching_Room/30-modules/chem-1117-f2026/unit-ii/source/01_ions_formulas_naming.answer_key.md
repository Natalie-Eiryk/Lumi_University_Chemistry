## LUMI-U2-Q01-A Q1a

**Answer:** An atom or bonded group of atoms with a net electric charge.

**Why:** Unequal total positive and negative charge makes the species an ion.

## LUMI-U2-Q01-B Q1b

**Answer:** A negatively charged ion.

**Why:** For a monatomic ion formed from a neutral atom, gaining electrons produces the negative charge.

## LUMI-U2-Q01-C Q1c

**Answer:** A positively charged ion.

**Why:** For a monatomic ion formed from a neutral atom, losing electrons produces the positive charge.

## LUMI-U2-Q01-D Q1d

**Answer:** Electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions.

**Why:** Electron transfer may form ions; the attractive interaction is the bond.

## LUMI-U2-Q01-E Q1e

**Answer:** An electrically neutral compound composed of cations and anions.

**Why:** The charges balance across the ion ratio; this does not require the individual ions to be neutral.
