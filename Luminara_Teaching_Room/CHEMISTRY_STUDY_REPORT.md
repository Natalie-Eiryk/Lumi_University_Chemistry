# Chemistry study clarity and naming focus — 1.8.0

Release **1.8.0** is installed, sealed and selected for the next launch, generation
**9**, previous selection **1.7.0**. The same desktop shortcut and root executable
remain in place. No user server was stopped or replaced by this work. Final
read-only inspection found no configured-port listener or campus health response.

## What changed

Chemistry now begins with **How to study** for new notebooks; existing activity
selections remain readable. The campus Today card and Chemistry entrance give
ion naming, charges, compound names and Roman numerals a prominent place.
Earlier Unit I vocabulary is labeled separately from ion naming.

Ion Language has six short sets of six existing questions: ion names/charges,
naming endings/families, compound name to formula, formula to name, Roman numerals,
and mixed repair. A five-session plan combines both naming directions for this
week. The complete 72-question bank remains available. Ion recall offers a core
12-ion set, six variable-metal cards and all 62 original cards; selectors do not
reveal the names during reverse recall.

The common routine is **Recall → check → explain → revisit**. Worksheet questions
and ion practice use consistent previous/next controls, hints and worked-answer
language. Explanation questions explicitly offer self-review instead of claiming
automatic correctness. Short summaries introduce all six naming lessons; full
original lesson text, worked examples and teaching notes remain accessible.

After a recorded check, **Start a fresh answer** starts another attempt while
retaining prior history and support evidence. It refuses to clear unfinished
drafts. Switching sets preserves responses, and delayed feedback cannot attach
to a newly selected question. Progress counts questions with recorded checks,
including those needing revision; it is neither an accuracy nor mastery score.

All work uses the existing shared Save/journal boundary and unchanged Chemistry
and Ion Language notebook keys. No additional persistence store, launcher,
interpreted backend or hosted PDF viewer was introduced. Native C++ compilation
admits the new presentation script through the existing public-resource map.
